StudentStack privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Who we are

StudentStack (“we”) is an app that gathers student discounts in one place. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, who helps us run it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

Questions or requests: knowit.app.me@gmail.com. This address also reaches the person in charge of protecting personal information at StudentStack.

What we collect

Guest use: you can use StudentStack without an account. We give your phone a random user ID so your choices (country, school, saved offers) work. We don’t ask for your name or email.

Account (optional): your email address, to send you a one-time sign-in code, and the first name you choose to add.

Your answers: your country (Canada, United States or Other) and, in Canada, your school — to show only the offers you can claim.

What you do with offers: the offers you save, the offers whose code or link you open (and when), and whether you then went to the brand’s site. We use this to show your codes again, to prevent abuse of limited codes, and to rank “Popular this week” (counted across all students, never shown individually).

What you send us: deals you share (brand, discount, description, category, link, end date and an optional screenshot you pick) and problems you report on an offer (reason and optional note).

Reminders: whether you turned on expiry reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don’t collect a device or push token.

Technical logs: like any online service, our hosting provider processes your IP address and request logs to deliver and secure the service.

What we don’t do

We don’t use advertising or analytics tools, we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t sell or rent your personal information. We don’t collect your location, contacts or photos library (only the one screenshot you choose to attach to a shared deal).

We don’t share your information with the brands in the app. When you open an offer’s link, you leave StudentStack: the brand’s own website, terms and privacy policy then apply.

Who helps us run StudentStack

Supabase, Inc. hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Our email delivery provider sends sign-in codes to the address you enter. They process your information only on our behalf, to run the service, under contracts that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

Your information may be stored and processed outside your province or country, including in the United States, where local law may allow authorities to access it.

How long we keep it

Account information, saved offers, shared deals and reports: until you delete your account.

Guest information: until you delete it, or automatically after 30 days without using the app.

When you delete your account or guest data, we delete it from our systems right away. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups disappear as those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

Delete everything: Profile → “Delete my account” (or “Delete my data” as a guest). This removes your profile, saved offers, revealed-code history, reports and shared deals, including screenshots.

Reminders: turn them off in Profile at any time, or in your phone’s Settings.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to knowit.app.me@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your privacy regulator (in Québec, the Commission d’accès à l’information; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner).

Security

Your information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only their own saved offers, reports and shared deals, and discount codes are only handed out by our server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

StudentStack is for students aged 13 and older and isn’t directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 gave us personal information, write to knowit.app.me@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date below and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
