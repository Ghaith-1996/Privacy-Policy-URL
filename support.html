StudentStack support

What StudentStack does

StudentStack gathers student discounts in one place so you can find the ones you can claim where you study, save them, get a reminder before they end, and see the conditions and steps before you leave the app.

Independent and free

StudentStack is independent: we are not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by the brands shown. Brand names and logos belong to their owners and are only used to identify each offer.

StudentStack is free. We don’t sell anything in the app, and we don’t earn a commission on these offers.

How offers are checked

Each offer comes from the brand’s official page or its student-verification partner (Student Beans, UNiDAYS, SheerID, ID.me, GitHub Education…). Every offer page shows when we last checked it.

Brands set their own offers and can change or end them at any time: the brand’s terms always apply. Most offers ask for proof that you are a student; StudentStack doesn’t verify your status and can’t guarantee that you will qualify.

Something wrong? Use “Report a problem” on the offer and we will fix or remove it.
